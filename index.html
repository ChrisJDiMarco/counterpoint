THE ART OF ARRIVING TOGETHER

Conduct the machine.

You’re amber. Your three lights must occupy the three amber rings together. The rings are bells, not parking spaces.

01

Choose a switch

Click a junction to preview its other setting. Straight sends north → south and west → east. Turned sends north → east and west → south.

02

Respect the light

You may change an empty junction or one with your light at an entrance. A junction with only opposing lights is protected. Mixed colors let either player change it.

03

Let the beat happen

Press “Flip & advance.” All six lights travel one junction simultaneously. Tracks leaving an edge return at the opposite edge along the same row or column.

04

Make a perfect chord

Win when your three lights fill your three bells after any beat, even your opponent’s. Both colors finishing together, or three exact repetitions, is a draw.

A longer route can be the better route. Use a detour to bring an early light into rhythm with the others.

Keyboard: Tab to a switch, Enter to select. Enter again to advance. Esc clears the preview. V changes view. ⌘Z undoes your last turn.
