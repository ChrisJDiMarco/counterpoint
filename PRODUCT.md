# Counterpoint for Mac

Authorized by Chris: make the proposed Counterpoint game now as an Electron Mac app, playable in 3D and visually exceptional.

Acceptance: A packaged, locally signed Apple-silicon Counterpoint.app opens a polished original 3D clockwork board, supports real orbit/zoom and switch interaction, and runs the complete proposed game against a computer or a local second player.

Scope: original twelve-switch 4x3 directed loop board; six lights; protected opponent-only junctions; simultaneous beats; three bells per color; win and repetition draw; preview before commitment; undo, restart, rules; computer opponent; local saved game; sound and motion controls. The renderer uses original procedural geometry, with no third-party model or network dependency at runtime.

Visual direction: precision instrument in a dark observatory. Champagne metal, deep teal ceramic, luminous amber and ice-blue glass, fine engraving, gentle bloom, tactile switches, clear track direction and illuminated motion. The game board is the dominant surface. Interface remains quiet and legible.

Build: isolated source folder; pure rules plus meaningful tests; Three.js renderer and compact HTML UI; secure Electron loopback shell; Apple-silicon packaging and ad-hoc signing. Verify deterministic rules, AI legality, owner-visible packaged window, real pointer interaction, move/AI/undo/restart, and camera controls.

Constraints: no changes to unrelated projects; no accounts or backend; no claim of proven game balance or master-level AI. Stop only for destructive changes outside the new app or missing access that prevents verification.
